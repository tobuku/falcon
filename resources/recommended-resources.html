Why These Resources?

Falconry is a hands-on pursuit. You build your own mews, maintain a weathering yard, manage land, and spend most of your time outdoors. The resources below support that lifestyle — from woodworking plans for raptor housing to self-sufficiency guides for the property around it.

We only recommend resources we'd point a fellow falconer toward. Nothing on this page is filler.
